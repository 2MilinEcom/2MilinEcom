I build iOS apps, web tools, and automations. I use this profile to share the projects I have been working on and explain how I put them together.

* [Bible Companion](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/bible-companion-portfolio): I built a SwiftUI Bible study app with reading journeys, companion conversations, appearance settings, and home screen widgets.
* [NextPlay Social Studio](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/nextplay-social-studio-portfolio): I built a video and social content workspace for editing clips, preparing captions, reviewing posts, and scheduling publication.
* [VibeProof](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/vibeproof-portfolio): I built a product reviews app with storefront widgets, merchant moderation, review requests, and clearly labeled AI previews.
* [Deal Finder](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/deal-finder): I built an iOS app for finding nearby offers, comparing savings opportunities, and keeping receipts with a Python backend.
* [Bloom](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/bloom-cycle-tracker): I built a private iOS cycle and symptom journal with calendar views, charts, device authentication, and local storage.
* [Auto Work](https://github.com/2MilinEcom/auto-work): I built a career dashboard to keep applications, interview preparation, scheduling, and reply drafts in one place.
